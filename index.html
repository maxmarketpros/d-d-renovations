Meet D&D Renovations

Family-owned remodeling across Northwest Indiana

Daniel Vasa and his father run a remodeling business built around reliable communication and showing up on time.

D&D Renovations is a father-and-son, family-owned business serving Crown Point and the wider Northwest Indiana area. Daniel Vasa and his father describe more than 30 years of experience, a focus on showing up on time, reliable service, and free estimates. The business states that it is licensed and bonded.

We work on the parts of a home people use and depend on every day: kitchens, bathrooms, basements, decks, roofing, siding, concrete, and home additions. A project starts with listening to what is not working in the current space, then discussing the scope and the choices that matter.
