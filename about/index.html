The business story

Daniel Vasa and a family approach to remodeling

D&D Renovations describes itself as father-and-son owned.

Daniel Vasa and his father run D&D Renovations as a family-owned business serving Crown Point and communities across Northwest Indiana. They describe more than 30 years of experience. Their approach is to listen first, show up on time, and focus on the home in front of them.

The services cover kitchens, bathrooms, basements, decks, roofing, siding, concrete, basement water solutions, home additions, and wider home renovation. A homeowner can come with a single room problem or a larger plan. The business offers free estimates and states that it is licensed and bonded.
